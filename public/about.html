About ShaadiTemplate

ShaadiTemplate helps families make wedding invitations that look as grand as the wedding itself. Pick a design, fill in your families' names, dates and venues, and download images for WhatsApp or a print-ready PDF for the press.

Invitations come in English, Hindi, Marathi, Gujarati, Punjabi and Tamil, for every event from Haldi and Mehendi to Sangeet, the wedding and the reception. Phonetic typing lets you write in your own script with an English keyboard.

There is no login. Your draft stays in your own browser.

ShaadiTemplate is made and run by Ashok Lalakiya in Surat, India. Questions or ideas are welcome on the contact page.
