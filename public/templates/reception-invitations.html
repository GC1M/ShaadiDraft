Reception invitations

The reception is often hosted by the groom's family, or by both families together, and it is the event colleagues and wider acquaintances are invited to. Keep it short and formal. The invocation and long family lists usually stay on the wedding card. Give the date, time, venue and the hosts' names, and mention dinner.

Because reception guests include people outside the family, an English card is common even when the wedding card is in Hindi, Gujarati or Tamil.

Every design below opens in the editor with sample wording for this ceremony. You can switch any card to English, Hindi, Marathi, Gujarati, Punjabi or Tamil once it is open.
