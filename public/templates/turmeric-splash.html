About this design

Warm cream paper with soft gold patches, like faint turmeric stains, inside a thin gold double frame. Gold leafy flourishes curl in from all four corners, and a small sprig of mango leaves hangs at the top centre. At the bottom sits a wide brass urli filled with turmeric paste, a few white flowers floating on it, with leaves tucked in on either side. Everything is painted in soft gold and olive, so the card reads as calm rather than bright.

A small lotus emblem sits above Haldi Ceremony, set in turmeric-orange Pinyon Script. The invitation line reads Join us for a morning of turmeric and laughter, and the names are in olive script with a small 'with' between them. The date is in Cinzel capitals, then the time, the venue in bold serif, the city and an RSVP block with both families. There is no religious line.

It is calmer than most Haldi cards, which suits a small morning ceremony at home. The sample venue is a hotel in Udaipur; for a Haldi at home, put your house name and area instead, and add a line about the dress code if you are asking guests to wear yellow. Compared with Marigold and Haldi Sunshine, the other English Haldi card, which is bright butter yellow with a marigold toran, Turmeric Splash is paler and quieter. Families who want a Ganesha line and Hindi text can use Haldi Kalash Saffron.
