Hindi shadi cards and invitation designs

In Hindi the wedding card is usually just the shaadi card (शादी कार्ड), or more formally the vivah nimantran patra (विवाह निमंत्रण पत्र). Most open with ॥ श्री गणेशाय नमः ॥ and the Vakratunda shlok, then शुभ विवाह, the hosts' line, the couple written as चि. with सुपुत्री and सुपुत्र lines, the baraat and phere times, and a निवेदक or दर्शनाभिलाषी list of the relatives who are inviting you. On a Hindi card the reception is often called प्रीतिभोज.

The designs below are the ones whose sample text is written in Hindi, grouped by ceremony. The wedding cards have six pages: cover, functions, wedding-day programme, letter, family names and a closing message. The other ceremonies are a single card. Any design can be switched to another language in the editor, and any other design can be switched to Hindi, which fills in the Hindi sample wording.

With phonetic typing on, type names and places in English letters (ananya, sharma parivar) and the editor turns each word into Devanagari as you type. Press Esc to keep a word in English.

For the wording itself, read Hindi shadi card matter and the wording guide for every ceremony.
