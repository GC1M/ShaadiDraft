About this design

Chocolate brown with a soft sheen across the middle, framed by a thin gold double line with a small diamond at the top centre. Gold filigree scrolls curl into all four corners. At the bottom a wide brass thali glows, with a lit diya in the middle and a small mound on each side of it, one reddish and one green.

The text opens with ॥ શ્રી ગણેશાય નમઃ ॥ and a gold Om emblem, then સગાઈ સમારંભ set large in gold. The names are in gold with ચિ. before each, joined by સંગ, and the parents' lines are in small cream type under them. The date, an evening time, a party plot on Ring Road in Surat and the નિમંત્રક block with two names from the bride's side close the card, with all the numerals in Gujarati.

Gol Dhana is what many Gujarati families call the engagement, after the jaggery and coriander seeds shared to mark it, and the brass thali at the bottom carries that idea. It suits Gujarati Hindu families holding a sagai at a party plot, hall or home, and the dark brown and gold looks rich on an evening card. Compared with Misri Roka Phulkari, the Punjabi engagement card that is also named after a sweet, Gol Dhana is darker and has a Ganesha line where Misri Roka has Ek Onkar. Mandapam Mangalashtak is the Marathi engagement card with the same Ganesha line and Om emblem, and Pithi Mirror Work is the Gujarati card for the turmeric ceremony.
