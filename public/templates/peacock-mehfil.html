About this design

A deep peacock teal fills the card, with a soft sheen running diagonally across it. A thin gold line frames the edge, and inside it a tall gold arch rises to a point near the top, like a jharokha window, with a string of small gold rings and beads draped along its curve and a little medallion above the tip. From the bottom edge a half fan of peacock feathers opens upwards, each eye drawn in blue, teal and gold on fine gold stems.

The text sits inside the arch in cream and gold. A gold lotus emblem tops Mehendi Ceremony in Pinyon Script, the names Ananya and Arjun follow in the same script, and the date is in Marcellus, with the time, venue and RSVP in a plain serif. The sample invites guests to 'an afternoon of henna, music and colour', and there is no religious line. The venue is a placeholder hotel in Udaipur, so change it to your own, and change the time if your Mehendi runs into the evening.

Peacocks are a classic Mehendi motif and the teal sits well beside green henna. The card shares its English wording with Emerald Henna Courtyard; that one is a darker emerald with henna scallops and a half mandala, while this one has the arch and the feathers, so it looks more ornate on a phone screen. If the evening is a sangeet with bhajans rather than a Mehendi, Vrindavan Peacock & Flute also has a peacock feather, with Hindi text and a Radha-Krishna invocation.
