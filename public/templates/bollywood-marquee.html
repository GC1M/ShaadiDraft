About this design

Near-black chocolate with a gold frame lined with small glowing bulbs on all four sides, like the lights around an old cinema sign. At the top is a gold art deco sunburst, a half circle of fine rays above a short bar, and along the bottom runs a gold strip of small rectangles like a reel of film.

A gold ring with a lotus sits under the sunburst. Sangeet Night is set large in gold Bodoni Moda, a high-contrast serif with the look of a film poster title. The names Ananya and Arjun are in gold Pinyon Script, the date is in Cinzel capitals, and the rest is in a cream serif. There is no religious line. The sample invites guests to an evening of music and dance from 7:30 pm, and the venue is a placeholder hotel in Udaipur.

Pick it if your Sangeet has rehearsed performances and a dance floor, and you want the card to look like a night out rather than a ritual. If family members are performing, add a line saying so, so that guests arrive on time. Compared with Black Tie Emerald Deco, the reception card that shares the art deco sunburst, Bollywood Marquee is darker and more playful, with the bulbs and the film strip doing the work. For a music evening before a Nikkah, Qawwali Mehfil Lanterns has the same English text under a Bismillah line, and Gujarati families may prefer Garba Raas Night.
