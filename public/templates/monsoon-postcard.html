About this design

The card is made to look like a postcard stamp. Its outer edge is cut in small perforations, with a thin double silver line inside. The background is slate blue-grey, darker towards the bottom, with faint diagonal rain lines in the upper half. At the top sits a round postmark with an umbrella in the middle, flanked by two sets of wavy lines like a franking mark, and near the bottom edge a few thin ripple rings spread out as if rain has landed in a puddle.

All the text is in white and pale silver. A small lotus emblem sits above Save the Date in Pinyon Script, then 'Kindly save the date for the wedding of', the names Ananya and Arjun in the same script, and the date 22 . 11 . 2026 in Cinzel. The sample adds 'Formal invitation to follow', a place, and an RSVP line with the families' names and a phone number. There is no religious line. The place is a placeholder in Udaipur, so replace it with your own city or venue.

It suits a wedding between July and September, or a couple who met in the rain, and it is one of the quieter save the dates in the set. Celestial Moon Phases is the other dark English save the date, navy with stars and gold; Monsoon is cooler and greyer, with silver in place of gold and a playful postcard idea behind it. For a light card with the same English text, see Ivory Monogram Minimal.
