About this design

The card is deep navy blue, a little lighter near the bottom, scattered with tiny white stars in an even pattern. A thin gold frame runs round it, with small four-point gold stars at the middle of the top and bottom edges. Across the top is a row of seven moon phases in gold, from a thin crescent through half and full and back again. Near the bottom right sits a large gold crescent, with three small gold stars hanging on thin threads beside it.

The text is in gold and cream. A small gold lotus emblem sits above Save the Date in Pinyon Script, followed by 'Kindly save the date for the wedding of', the names Ananya and Arjun in the same script, and the date in Cinzel. The sample ends with 'Formal invitation to follow', a placeholder place in Udaipur and an RSVP line with the families' names and a phone number. There is no religious line, so the card works for any family, and the dark background keeps the gold text easy to read on a phone.

It suits an evening or night wedding, or a couple who chose the date by the moon. Monsoon Postcard has the same wording on a slate blue card with a stamp-cut edge, rain and silver; Celestial is darker and warmer, with gold on navy and a more formal feel. For a light, quiet card with the same English text, see Ivory Monogram Minimal, and for a navy reception card later in the wedding, Palace Night Lights has a similar night sky with Hindi text.
