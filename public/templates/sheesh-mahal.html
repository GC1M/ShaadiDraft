About this design

The card is plum purple with a sheen across the middle and a thin silver double frame. Along the top runs a band of silver triangles in light and dark shades, like cut mirror pieces, and silver leafy flourishes curl in from all four corners. From the bottom edge rises a half fan of the same faceted mosaic, rings of small silver and grey triangles catching the light, after the mirrored halls of Rajasthani forts.

The text is in white and pale lilac. It opens with ॥ श्री गणेशाय नमः ॥ and a ringed Om emblem, then the heading प्रीतिभोज in large Amita letters. The host line reads शर्मा एवं मेहता परिवार नवदंपति के प्रीतिभोज में आपकी उपस्थिति की कामना करते हैं. The names चि. अनन्या and चि. अर्जुन have their parents' lines beneath, and the date, the time रात्रि 8 बजे से, the venue and the निवेदक block follow in Tiro Devanagari Hindi. The venue is a placeholder hotel in Udaipur. If only one family hosts the reception, change the host line and the निवेदक block.

It suits a dressy evening reception at a hotel or banquet hall, for North Indian families who still want a Ganesha line on the card. Palace Night Lights is the other Hindi reception card with the same wording; it is navy with a string of lights and a palace silhouette, while Sheesh Mahal is all plum and silver with no buildings. For an English reception card in a similarly rich colour, see Black Tie Emerald Deco.
