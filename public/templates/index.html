Invitation designs by ceremony

ShaadiTemplate has a separate set of designs for each event of the wedding, from the first save-the-date to the reception. Each design is a complete card with sample wording written for that ceremony, in English, Hindi, Marathi, Gujarati, Punjabi or Tamil. Pick the ceremony below to see every design for it, what makes each one different, and which families it suits.

Save the date cards

6 designs. A save-the-date goes out months before the wedding card, often before the venue or the full schedule is fixed.

Engagement and ring ceremony invitations

5 designs. The engagement goes by many names: sagai, roka, mangni, sakharpuda, Gol Dhana, nischayathartham.

Haldi invitations

6 designs. Haldi, Pithi in Gujarati homes and Mangala Snanam in Tamil ones, is the turmeric ceremony held a day or two before the wedding.

Mehendi invitations

5 designs. The Mehendi is an afternoon or evening gathering where the bride, and often every woman in the family, has henna applied.

Sangeet invitations

5 designs. The Sangeet is the party of the wedding week: songs, choreographed dances, and in Gujarati families often a full night of garba and raas.

Wedding invitation cards

13 designs. The wedding card is the formal one and carries the most information: the invocation, both families, the muhurat, the baraat or jaan arrival, the venue, and the names of the relatives who are inviting you.

Reception invitations

6 designs. The reception is often hosted by the groom's family, or by both families together, and it is the event colleagues and wider acquaintances are invited to.
