About this design

A navy night sky scattered with a grid of tiny white stars, inside a thin gold frame, with a small crescent moon at the top centre. A string of warm bulbs swings in two loops across the top. Along the bottom stands a palace silhouette in deep navy outlined in gold: a large central dome flanked by smaller domed pavilions with finials, every window glowing amber, and a soft gold haze rising behind it.

The text opens with ॥ श्री गणेशाय नमः ॥, then an Om emblem in a gold ring and प्रीतिभोज, the formal Hindi word for the reception dinner, set large in gold. The host line says the Sharma and Mehta families hope for your presence at the newly married couple's reception. Both names have चि. before them, with the parents' names beneath in small grey type, followed by the date, रात्रि 8 बजे से, the venue and the निवेदक block.

It suits an evening reception at a heritage hotel or banquet hall, especially when many of the guests read Hindi. If the groom's family is hosting alone, change the host line and the निवेदक block to match. Compared with Sheesh Mahal Mirror Night, which uses the same Hindi heading on plum with silver mirror mosaic, Palace Night is darker and calmer. For an English reception card, Black Tie Emerald Deco is the formal hotel option and Garden Fairy Lights the softer one.
