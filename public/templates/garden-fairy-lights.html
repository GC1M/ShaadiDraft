About this design

Pale sage green, slightly lighter at the centre, inside a thin gold frame. A string of fairy lights with small warm bulbs runs in a gentle curve across the top, with round eucalyptus leaves on twigs at both ends. Two more eucalyptus sprigs rise from the bottom corners in grey-green and sage, so the middle of the card stays clear for the text.

Below a small lotus emblem, Grand Reception is set in gold Pinyon Script. The host line has the Sharma and Mehta families requesting the pleasure of your company, and the names Ananya and Arjun are in dark green script with D/o and S/o lines in small grey serif. The date is in Marcellus, then the time, the venue in bold serif, the city and an RSVP block. There is no religious line.

It is soft and outdoorsy, made for a lawn or garden reception in the evening. The sample venue is a hotel in Udaipur, so replace it with your own lawn or garden, and if the groom's family is hosting alone, shorten the host line and the RSVP. Compared with Champagne Minimal, which is plainer and more formal, Garden Fairy Lights has more picture and a lighter mood; Black Tie Emerald Deco sits at the other end, for a formal hotel reception. Muslim families who want a calm English layout with Bismillah can use Nikkah and Walima Ivory Gold.
