About this design

The card is deep navy blue with a soft light in the middle and a thin gold double frame. Gold leafy flourishes curl in from all four corners. Across the top hang three small diamond-shaped lanterns on a gold string, each with a white light at its centre. At the bottom sits a garbo, the round clay pot with a lamp burning on its lid, its body ringed with rows of gold dots, and a pair of crossed dandiya sticks stands in each bottom corner.

The text is in gold and white. It opens with ॥ શ્રી ગણેશાય નમઃ ॥ and a ringed Om emblem, then the heading સંગીત સંધ્યા in large Farsan letters and the line સંગીત અને ગરબાની રંગત માણવા પધારશોજી. The names ચિ. કૃપા and ચિ. હર્ષ are joined by સંગ, and the date, the time રાત્રે ૮:૦૦ કલાકે, the venue and the નિમંત્રક block follow in Noto Serif Gujarati. If the evening is only raas garba, you can change the heading to રાસ ગરબા. The venue is a placeholder party plot in Surat.

It suits Gujarati families holding a garba night before the wedding. It shares its names, Ganesha line and hosts with Pithi Mirror Work and Gulabi Kankotri, so the three can go out as a set. Bollywood Marquee Night is the English sangeet card for a dance floor and rehearsed performances, with marquee bulbs and a sunburst; Garba Raas Night is the one for dandiya in a party plot. For a Hindi sangeet with bhajans or raas, see Vrindavan Peacock & Flute.
