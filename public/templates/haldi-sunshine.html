About this design

The card is pale butter yellow at the top, deepening to a warm sunflower yellow at the bottom. A toran of marigold strings hangs from a green cord along the top edge, each string alternating orange and yellow flowers and ending in a small green leaf or a red bead. Along the bottom is a thick bank of orange and yellow marigolds with green leaves, and in the middle of it sits a small brass bowl of turmeric paste.

A small round emblem with a pink lotus sits above the title. Haldi Ceremony is set in rust-brown Great Vibes script, and the names Ananya and Arjun are in the same script, a little smaller. The invitation line, venue and RSVP are in a plain dark serif, with the date in a lighter brown. There is no invocation or shlok, so nothing on the card ties it to one religion. The sample line, a morning of turmeric and laughter, keeps the tone informal, and the venue is a placeholder hotel in Udaipur, so put in your own home or hall.

It suits families who want a bright, cheerful Haldi card in English that can go to friends of any background on WhatsApp. If you want guests in yellow, add a dress code line under the venue. Compared with Turmeric Splash, which is also English with no religious line, Haldi Sunshine is the louder of the two: Turmeric Splash is cream with a few mango leaves and a brass urli, while this card is yellow all over and framed with marigolds at the top and bottom. For a Hindi card with a Ganesha line, look at Haldi Kalash Saffron.
