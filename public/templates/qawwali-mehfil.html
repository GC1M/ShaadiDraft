About this design

Midnight navy, darker at the sides, with a tall Mughal multifoil arch drawn in a fine gold line. Its scalloped top rises to a point at the centre and its sides run straight down to the bottom. Three brass lanterns hang on chains from the top edge and glow warm orange, the middle one lowest and largest. Along the base sit three small arched niches drawn in the same gold line.

The text opens with Bismillah-ir-Rahman-ir-Rahim in small gold serif, then a gold ring with a star at its centre. Sangeet Night is set in gold Pinyon Script, the names in the same script, and the invitation line, date, time, venue and RSVP in a cream and gold serif. The rest of the card is left empty, so the lanterns and the arch stand out. The sample is the general English Sangeet wording, with the names Ananya and Arjun and the Sharma and Mehta families in the RSVP, so change those lines for your own family. If the evening is a qawwali rather than a dance night, change the title and the line about music and dance too, for example to Qawwali Night. The venue is a placeholder hotel in Udaipur.

It suits Muslim families who hold a music evening before the Nikkah, and pairs with Nikkah & Walima Ivory Gold, which shares the Bismillah line and has gold lanterns under a pointed arch on ivory. Compared with Bollywood Marquee Night, which has the same English text in gold on a dark ground, Qawwali Mehfil is calmer, with lanterns in place of cinema bulbs.
