About this design

Blush pink with a lighter glow at the centre, framed by a fine rose-gold line with rounded corners and a dotted inner rule. Near the top is a small rose-gold lotus emblem in a ring of rays. At the bottom, a solitaire ring sits in a pool of soft light: a rose-gold band seen at an angle, a faceted diamond drawn in outline above it, and two small sparkles beside the stone.

Ring Ceremony is set in large rose-brown Pinyon Script, and the names Ananya and Arjun are in the same script, each with a D/o or S/o line in small serif. The date is in Cinzel capitals, then the time, The Leela Palace in bold serif, the city, and an RSVP block with both families and one phone number. The sample is a joyful invitation from both families and has no religious line, so it works for any family.

It is the most modern of the ring ceremony cards, a good fit for an engagement party, a ring exchange at a hotel, or a couple announcing the engagement on social media. Compared with Ivory Monogram Minimal, which shares the script names and fine frame in ivory and gold, Rose Gold Solitaire is softer in colour and carries a picture of the ring. Families who want a traditional engagement card in their own language can look at Gol Dhana Brass Thali in Gujarati or Misri Roka Phulkari in Punjabi, both with an invocation at the top.
