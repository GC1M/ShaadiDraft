About this design

The card is deep emerald green with a soft diagonal sheen, framed by two thin gold lines whose corners are notched in steps, the way art deco frames are. At the top a gold sunburst fans out from a half disc, its rays and arcs in fine lines, resting on a short gold bar with a small chevron below. At the bottom centre the frame rises in a small stepped block, like the top of a deco building.

Grand Reception is set large in gold Bodoni Moda, a high-contrast serif, under a small gold lotus emblem. The host line reads 'The Sharma and Mehta families request the pleasure of your company at the reception of', and the names Ananya and Arjun are in Pinyon Script with D/o and S/o lines in small serif type. The date is in Cinzel capitals, followed by the time, venue and RSVP. There is no religious line. The venue is a placeholder hotel in Udaipur, and if only one family is hosting, shorten the host line and the RSVP to match.

It reads as a black-tie card, which suits a formal hotel reception, a corporate guest list or friends abroad. Champagne Minimal is the other formal English reception card, but it is cream with a plain double gold frame and almost no art; pick Black Tie Emerald if you want colour and a bold heading. For an outdoor evening, Garden Fairy Lights is softer, and for a Hindi reception card with a Ganesha line, see Sheesh Mahal Mirror Night.
