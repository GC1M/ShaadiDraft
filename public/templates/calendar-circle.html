About this design

A plain cream card inside a thin maroon frame with scooped corners and a finer gold line just inside it. At the bottom is a small desk calendar: a maroon header with two gold rings on top, and a white page of dots in rows, one of them replaced by a small gold heart inside a hand-drawn maroon circle. Everything else on the card is space and text.

The text is all in maroon Devanagari. It opens with ॥ श्री गणेशाय नमः ॥ and a gold Om emblem, then तिथि याद रखें (remember the date) set large, and a line asking guests to keep the date free. The names follow with चि. before each, joined by संग, then the date with spaced dots, 21 . 11 . 2027. Under it, निमंत्रण पत्र शीघ्र tells guests the full card will follow, then the place and the निवेदक block with the family names and a mobile number. There is no venue name or time, which is what a save the date leaves out.

It suits Hindi-speaking families who want to send the date early on WhatsApp, before the full invitation is ready. The calendar is a fixed drawing and does not change with your date, so the date line in the text is what guests read. Compared with Ivory Monogram Minimal, the English save the date with no religious line, Calendar Circle is just as quiet but keeps the Ganesha line that most Hindi cards open with. Gujarati families can use Bandhani Festive Invite, which also opens with a Ganesha line.
