About this design

Ivory paper shading to a soft parchment at the bottom, with a tall pointed arch running the full height of the card, drawn as a gold outer line and a green inner line. Five gold lanterns of different lengths hang from the top edge, the middle one lowest. At the bottom is a line drawing of a mosque in pale sand and gold: a central dome with a crescent finial, two smaller domes, a row of arched windows and a minaret on each side.

The text opens with Bismillah-ir-Rahman-ir-Rahim in green, then a crescent and star emblem and Walima Reception in large gold Pinyon Script. The names Zoya and Ayaan are in green script with D/o and S/o lines, and the date, time, venue and RSVP follow in a plain serif. The sample names both families as hosts; if the groom's family is hosting the Walima alone, shorten the host line and the RSVP.

The template is filed under Reception because the sample is written for the Walima, the reception after the Nikkah, and it is a single-page card. To use it for the Nikkah itself, change the title to Nikkah Ceremony, and change the host line, date and time; the arch, lanterns and mosque work for both. The sample venue is a placeholder hotel in Udaipur, so replace it with your own hall or lawn.

Stay in English for this card. If you switch it to Hindi, Gujarati or another language, the editor fills in that language's general reception sample, with different names and a different host line, and you would need to rewrite every line. It suits Muslim families in India and abroad who want a calm, formal English card, and pairs with Qawwali Mehfil Lanterns for a music evening before the Nikkah.
