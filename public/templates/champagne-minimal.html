About this design

Champagne cream, a little warmer at the edges, inside a thin double gold frame. The only other marks are a short gold line with a small diamond near the top, a matching short line and dot near the bottom, and a small gold ring with a lotus above the title. There is no border pattern, no flowers and no illustration.

Grand Reception is set in gold Pinyon Script, and the names Ananya and Arjun are in dark brown script, with the D/o and S/o lines in a small muted serif under them. The host line names both families, who request the pleasure of your company at the reception. The date is in dark brown Cinzel capitals, followed by an 8:00 pm start, the venue and the RSVP. There is no religious line, and the venue is a placeholder hotel in Udaipur.

A good choice when the reception is formal, at a hotel or banquet hall, and you want the wording, not the decoration, to carry it. Because there is so little on it, it also reads well for colleagues and wider acquaintances, who are often invited to the reception only. If the groom's family is hosting alone, change the host line and the RSVP. Compared with Black Tie Emerald Deco, which is also English with no religious line but has an emerald ground and an art deco sunburst, Champagne Minimal is lighter and quieter. Ivory Monogram Minimal is a save the date in the same calm style.
