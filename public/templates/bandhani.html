About this design

The whole card is deep red covered in tiny white dots set in an even grid, the look of bandhani tie-dye. Across the top is a green band with a row of white dots and a yellow saw-tooth edge pointing down into the red. The bottom mirrors it: a yellow saw-tooth edge pointing up, then a green band with a row of small lit diyas along the base.

The text is in yellow and white. It opens with ॥ શ્રી ગણેશાય નમઃ ॥ and a small Om emblem in a ringed circle, then the heading તારીખ યાદ રાખજો in large yellow Farsan letters and the line આ શુભ દિવસ અમારા માટે યાદ રાખજો. The names ચિ. કૃપા and ચિ. હર્ષ are in yellow with સંગ between them. Below a short divider come the date, the line કંકોત્રી હવે પછી, which tells guests the full kankotri will follow, the area and city, and the નિમંત્રક block with two names and a phone number, all in Noto Serif Gujarati. Because it only fixes the day, the sample gives a locality in Surat rather than a venue and has no time; add them if you already know.

It suits Gujarati families who want the first message about the wedding to look festive, with the same red and the same Ganesha line as the kankotri that follows. Uttarayan Kite Festival is the other Gujarati save the date, with a purple dusk sky, one kite and old-city rooftops; it is calmer and suits a winter wedding, while Bandhani is the brighter of the two. For a Hindi save the date with a Ganesha line, see Calendar Circle.
