About this design

The card is a twilight sky, deep navy at the top fading through teal to a soft peach glow just above the treeline. A curtain of green leaves runs along the top edge, and nine small brass temple bells hang from it on gold chains at different heights. At the bottom is a row of round dark green trees, then a lotus pond with lily pads, three pink lotuses and a peacock feather standing at each side.

The text opens with ॥ श्री राधे कृष्ण ॥ in small gold type, then a round emblem with a flute and a peacock feather, and संगीत संध्या set large in gold. The names, each with चि. before it, are in gold and larger than the rest. The date and time are in gold, and the venue and the निवेदक block are in white. There is no painted figure of Radha or Krishna anywhere on the card: the invocation, the flute and the feathers carry the theme, so the page stays quiet and easy to read on a phone.

It is made for Krishna-devotee families, and for a Sangeet with bhajans or raas rather than a DJ night. The sample invites guests to an evening of music and dance hosted by both families; if only one side is hosting, shorten the निवेदक line. Compared with Garba Raas Night, which is also a navy evening card but in Gujarati with lanterns and a garbo, Vrindavan is in Hindi and built around Radha and Krishna. For a Sangeet with rehearsed dances and a dance floor, Bollywood Marquee Night is the louder choice.
