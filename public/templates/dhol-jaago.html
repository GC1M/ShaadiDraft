About this design

Deep maroon, lighter at the centre, inside a thin gold border. Across the top runs a phulkari-style band: a row of gold diamonds, each with a smaller diamond inside, over a zigzag edge. At the bottom a dhol lies on its side, with cream drum heads, a maroon body laced with gold rope and a gold strap, flanked by two small lit diyas and gold star flowers in the corners.

The Punjabi text opens with ੴ ਸਤਿ ਨਾਮੁ and an Ek Onkar emblem, then ਸੰਗੀਤ ਸ਼ਾਮ in large gold letters. The invitation line asks guests to an evening of songs, giddha and bhangra, ਗੀਤਾਂ, ਗਿੱਧੇ ਅਤੇ ਭੰਗੜੇ ਦੀ ਸ਼ਾਮ. The names Simran Kaur and Harpreet Singh are in gold, then the date, ਸ਼ਾਮ 7:30 ਵਜੇ, the venue and a ਦਰਸ਼ਨ ਅਭਿਲਾਖੀ block with two names and a phone number.

Jaago is the Punjabi night procession where relatives dance through the streets with a lit pot and a dhol. The sample is written as a Sangeet; to use the card for the jaago itself, change the heading to ਜਾਗੋ and the invitation line to one about coming out with the nanke, as shown in the Punjabi wording guide. The sample venue is a gurdwara, carried over from the Punjabi wedding text, so replace it with the house, lawn or hall where the dancing will be. Compared with Mayun Night Parandi, the green Mehendi card with parandi tassels, Dhol Jaago is warmer and louder. For an English Sangeet with rehearsed performances and a dance floor, Bollywood Marquee Night is the closer match.
