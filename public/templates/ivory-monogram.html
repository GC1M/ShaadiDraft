About this design

Warm ivory paper with a fine grain, inside a thin double gold frame with a dotted inner rule and small gold diamonds at the top and bottom centre. Gold flourishes curl into all four corners. Near the top, a gold lace medallion sits between two long ornamental rules, and two gold laurel sprigs sweep in from the bottom corners to meet under the text.

Below a small gold lotus emblem, Save the Date is set in gold Pinyon Script, with the names Ananya and Arjun in the same script in dark brown and a small gold 'with' between them. The date is in bold serif as 21 . 11 . 2027, followed by Formal invitation to follow, the city, and an RSVP block in Cinzel capitals with both families and one phone number. The sample gives only the city, so guests can plan travel; the hall and timings go on the main invitation.

There is no religious line anywhere on the card, which makes it a good choice for an English save the date sent to friends, colleagues and family abroad. Compared with Champagne Minimal, which keeps a thin gold frame and drops nearly everything else, Ivory Monogram adds the medallion and laurels for a more finished look. If you would rather have the date drawn as the centrepiece, Calendar Circle does that in Hindi, and Celestial Moon Phases suits an evening or night wedding in English.
