About this design

The card is a deep emerald green, a little lighter in the middle, as if lit from above. Along the top runs a row of small gold dots over a wavy gold line that dips into scallops, with a gold curl hanging below each dip. At the bottom sits a half mandala of three rings of leaf shapes, alternating pale gold and copper, with gold spirals curling out on either side, like a henna pattern drawn on a courtyard floor.

The text is centred in cream and gold. A small gold lotus emblem sits above Mehendi Ceremony, set in Pinyon Script, and the names Ananya and Arjun are in the same script with a small 'with' between them. Under a thin divider come the date in Marcellus, then the time, venue and RSVP in a plain serif. There is no religious line, so the card works for any family. The sample invites guests to 'an afternoon of henna, music and colour', which fits a daytime Mehendi; if yours is in the evening, change that line and the time. The venue is a placeholder hotel in Udaipur, so put your own home, lawn or hall there.

It suits families who want an English Mehendi card that feels festive without being busy, since the art stays at the top and bottom edges and the middle is plain green. Peacock Teal Mehfil opens with the same English wording but frames it in a gold arch over a fan of peacock feathers, so pick that one for a more decorated card and this one for a calmer look. For a Hindi Mehendi card in a similar deep green, see Jharokha Jaal.
