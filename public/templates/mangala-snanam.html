About this design

Turmeric orange, shading to a deeper brown at the edges, inside a gold border cut into small temple teeth on all four sides. At the bottom sits a brass kalash with a coconut on mango leaves, with an orange lotus on each side of it, a gold palm frond leaning in from each lower corner and a pale band like water along the base.

The cover opens with ஓம் நமோ வேங்கடேசாய and the namam emblem of Venkateswara in a gold ring. மங்கள ஸ்நானம் is set large in cream, with the names below it, சௌ. before the bride's and சி. before the groom's. The date is a Saturday morning at 10:00, the day before the wedding on the Kanchipuram Temple Silk card, at the same kalyana mandapam in Mylapore, and both fathers sign the அன்புடன் அழைப்போர் block. There are no parents' lines under the names, so the text is shorter than on the engagement card.

The Mangala Snanam is for close family, so this card usually goes to relatives rather than the full guest list. If only one side is holding it, delete the other name and keep just that family in the closing block. It suits Tamil Hindu families who want the bath on its own card, and it matches Nischayathartham Banana Leaf and Kanchipuram Temple Silk, which share the invocation. Compared with Haldi Kalash Saffron, the Hindi Haldi card that also has a brass kalash at the bottom, Mangala Snanam is lighter in colour and has a temple-tooth border where Haldi Kalash has a marigold string.
