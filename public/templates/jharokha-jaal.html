About this design

The card is deep bottle green with a faint diagonal sheen. A gold band along the top edge carries a row of small squares, and two narrow gold lattice strips run down the left and right sides like carved stone screens. At the bottom stands a gold jharokha window: a small ribbed dome with a finial, a flat projecting ledge, and a pointed arch filled with fine diagonal jaal lattice. A row of small gold diamonds runs along the base.

The text is in gold and cream. A small gold lotus emblem sits above the heading मेहँदी रस्म in large Amita letters, followed by the line मेहँदी की रंगभरी रस्म में आप सादर आमंत्रित हैं. The names चि. अनन्या and चि. अर्जुन are in gold with संग between them, and the day and date, the time दोपहर 3 बजे से, the venue and the निवेदक block follow in Tiro Devanagari Hindi. There is no religious line, so the card works for any Hindi-speaking family. The venue is a placeholder hotel in Udaipur, so put your own there.

It suits Rajasthani and other North Indian families, and looks especially at home for a Mehendi at a haveli or heritage hotel. Emerald Henna Courtyard is the English Mehendi card in a similar green, with henna scallops and a half mandala instead of architecture; Jharokha Jaal is the more formal of the two. For the reception, Sheesh Mahal Mirror Night is also in Hindi and also takes its art from Rajasthani palaces, so the two sit well together.
