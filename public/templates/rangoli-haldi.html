About this design

Cream paper with a soft warm glow, framed by a fine red dotted border with a scalloped inner edge. A mango-leaf toran hangs across the top on a gold string, a single row of pointed green leaves. Rising from the bottom edge is a half rangoli in rings of petal shapes in red, mustard, green and gold, drawn in dotted strokes like poured rangoli powder, around a red centre.

The Marathi text opens with ॥ श्री गणेशाय नमः ॥ and an Om emblem in a maroon ring, then हळदी समारंभ in large maroon letters. The bride's name has चि.सौ.कां. before it and the groom's चि., both in olive green, joined by समवेत in maroon. The date and time use Marathi numerals, the sample venue is a mangal karyalay in Sadashiv Peth, Pune, and the card closes with आपले नम्र, two names and a phone number.

It is a good match if your Haldi is at home with a rangoli at the door, like the doorstep of a Marathi home on a festival morning. If the Haldi is at home rather than at a hall, replace the karyalay line with your address. Compared with Pithi Mirror Work, the Gujarati turmeric card with a mirror-dot border and a half mandala on mustard yellow, Rangoli Haldi is lighter and uses the Marathi forms of address. For the engagement in Marathi, Mandapam Mangalashtak is headed साखरपुडा and opens with the same Ganesha line.
