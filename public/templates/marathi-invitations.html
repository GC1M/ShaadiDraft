Marathi lagna patrika and invitation designs

A Marathi wedding card is a lagna patrika (लग्नपत्रिका). It usually opens with ॥ श्री गणेशाय नमः ॥, often alongside the family's kuladevata, then शुभविवाह and the names, with the bride written as चि.सौ.कां. and the groom as चि., and the parents named in the यांची कन्या and यांचा सुपुत्र form. The engagement is the साखरपुडा and the Haldi the हळदी समारंभ.

Two designs so far have Marathi as their own language, both for the ceremonies before the wedding, and they are listed below. There is no wedding design drawn specifically for Marathi families yet, but every wedding design can be switched to Marathi in the editor. When you switch, the editor fills in the full Marathi sample: शुभविवाह, the चि.सौ.कां. and चि. names, and six pages for the functions, the wedding-day programme, the letter, the family names and a closing message. Shubh Vivah Traditional, with its marigold toran and kalash, is a natural starting point.

With phonetic typing on, type names in English letters (mrunal, kulkarni) and the editor turns each word into Devanagari as you type. Press Esc to keep a word in English.

For the wording itself, read Marathi lagna patrika wording.
