About this design

The card is soft blush pink, warming to peach in the middle. A sage green band with a row of gold-outlined diamonds runs along the top edge, and a dashed gold line frames the page. At the bottom is a large eight-point star in pale sage with a gold outline, a ring of fine gold rays and a small gold diamond at its centre, after the geometric stars embroidered on phulkari shawls.

The text opens with ੴ ਸਤਿ ਨਾਮੁ in small green letters and a ringed Ek Onkar emblem in gold and maroon. The heading ਮੰਗਣੀ ਦੀ ਰਸਮ is set large in terracotta Baloo Paaji 2 letters. The names ਸਿਮਰਨ ਕੌਰ and ਹਰਪ੍ਰੀਤ ਸਿੰਘ are in green with their parents' lines beneath, joined by ਸੰਗ, and the date, the time ਸ਼ਾਮ 6:30 ਵਜੇ, the gurdwara venue in Ludhiana and the ਦਰਸ਼ਨ ਅਭਿਲਾਖੀ block follow in Tiro Gurmukhi. The sample is written for the mangni at a gurdwara. If your family calls the function the roka, or holds it at home or a hotel, change the heading and the venue lines.

It suits Sikh and Punjabi families who want a soft, light card for the engagement rather than the deep colours of the later functions. Anand Karaj Royal Blue carries the same Ek Onkar invocation for the wedding itself, and Dhol Jaago Phulkari and Mayun Night Parandi do the same for the jaago and mayun nights, so the cards can go out as a set. Dhol Jaago Phulkari also uses a phulkari pattern, but on deep maroon with a dhol; Misri Roka is the paler and quieter of the two.
